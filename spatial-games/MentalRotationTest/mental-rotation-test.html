Mental Rotation Test

Instructions

This is a test of your ability to recognize 3D objects from different viewpoints. You will be shown two 3D objects. Your task is to determine whether the object on the right is the same as the object on the left but rotated, or if it's a different (mirrored) object.

Press the "Same" button if the objects are identical but rotated, or the "Different" button if the objects are mirror images of each other.

The test consists of 10 trials. Try to respond as quickly and accurately as possible.
